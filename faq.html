FAQ.

Got questions about MediLog? We’ve got answers! Below are some of the most common inquiries from our users. If you don’t find what you’re looking for, feel free to reach out—we’re happy to help.

What is MediLog?

MediLog is a healthcare management app designed for families to seamlessly document and share important health information for their loved ones. From tracking medical history to recording doctor visits, MediLog keeps everything organized in one place.

Who can use MediLog?

MediLog is designed for individuals and families, especially those caring for aging parents, grandparents, or loved ones who need ongoing support.

MediLog is also designed for professional healthcare workers, where they need to care for multiple people at the same time and keep-up-to date with their latest health information.

What features does MediLog offer?

With MediLog, you can:

  • Document health incidents and updates
  • Track medication history
  • Record and review doctor’s visits
  • Share important care details with family members
How do I get started?

Simply download the MediLog app through the Google Play Store, then subscribe to an account and start adding health information. It’s easy to invite family members and collaborate on providing care.

What devices are supported?

Currently MediLog is only available for Android devices.

Pricing and Free Trial?

Each unique Google Play Store Account will initially get a free one-month trial, then it's only £4.99 per month afterwards. You can cancel any time.
